# Sub-processors and service providers

Last updated: 5 November 2026

*This is a translation for convenience. The German version is legally binding.*

**Sub-processors** under our [Data Processing Agreement](/avv) are listed in table 1. We notify customers of changes at least 30 days in advance by email.

## 1. Sub-processors

| Service | Company and seat | Purpose | Data | Transfer basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hosting | Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany | servers, database, backups (data centres in Germany) | all data stored by Askmetry | EU – no third-country transfer |
| ChatGPT API with web search | OpenAI Ireland Ltd, Dublin, Ireland | asking ChatGPT (measurement) | questions, brand and competitor names, any personal data contained therein | also processed in the USA by the OpenAI group: Standard Contractual Clauses |
| Claude API with web search | Anthropic Ireland, Limited, Dublin, Ireland | asking Claude (measurement); evaluating answers, brand profile, competitors, questions and actions | questions, brand/competitor details, content of the checked public website, AI answers | also processed in the USA by Anthropic, PBC: Standard Contractual Clauses |
| Gemini API with Google Search | Google Cloud EMEA Limited, Dublin, Ireland, with Google LLC, Mountain View, USA | asking Gemini (measurement) | questions, brand and competitor names | USA: EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Google LLC) and SCCs |
| Google results pages (AI Overviews, AI Mode) | DataForSEO OÜ, Tallinn, Estonia | retrieving Google search results for the questions | questions (search terms), country, language | EU company; onward transfers per DataForSEO via SCCs or adequacy decisions |
| Email delivery | Sendinblue SAS (Brevo), 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France | confirmation, result and report emails | email address, name, email content incl. PDF report | EU – no third-country transfer |
| DNS, bot protection | Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA | DNS; Turnstile bot protection for the free check | IP address, technical browser characteristics (Turnstile) | USA: EU-US Data Privacy Framework and SCCs |

Under their API terms the AI providers do not use submitted data to train their models; they may retain requests for a limited time for abuse monitoring and may pass search terms to search services for web search.
