# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

*This is a shortened translation for convenience. The German version is legally binding.*

## 1. Controller

lindotech GmbH, c/o Max Fabian Uth, Eppendorfer Weg 151, 20253 Hamburg, Germany. Managing director: Max Fabian Uth. Email: [hallo@askmetry.ai](mailto:hallo@askmetry.ai). We have not appointed a data protection officer as we are not legally required to.

## 2. In short

- We measure how brands appear in AI answers. To do so we read **public web pages** and send **questions to AI services**. Your name and email address are **not** sent to the AI services.
- **No analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.** No newsletter.
- Our servers are in **Germany** (Hetzner). Some providers are located in or process data in the USA, under the safeguards below.

## 3. Server logs

IP address, time, requested URL, status code, data volume, referrer and user agent. Purpose: delivery, stability, security. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Deleted after **14 days**, unless needed to investigate a specific security incident.

## 4. Free quick check

- **Website analysis:** Our crawler (user agent "Askmetry-SiteCheck") fetches public pages of the website you enter, plus files such as the site icon, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`. AI derives the brand profile, competitors and typical customer questions, which we then put to AI services and evaluate. Public pages may contain personal data (e.g. names in a legal notice); we do not target them. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for third-party data on public pages Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Results are reused for the same domain for up to **30 days** (without the original requester's contact data).
- **Email and double opt-in:** We send a confirmation link (valid 24 hours) and a "results ready" email. The confirmation code is stored only as a hash. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.
- **Abuse protection:** We limit checks per IP and per email. We store only a keyed hash of your IP address, never the address itself; deleted after **7 days**. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) checks that a human submits the form; it processes your IP address and browser characteristics and may read or store information in your browser. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR and Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary). Transfer: EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Retention:** Unconfirmed email addresses are deleted after **7 days**; without a purchase, email address and access data are deleted **90 days** after the check. Brand-level results (not linked to you) may be kept for caching and aggregate statistics.

## 5. Customer account

Email, name and password (stored only as a secure hash), plus your workspaces, brands, competitors, questions, results and reports. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Kept until you delete the account or ask us to; then deleted within **30 days**, and from backups within a further **30 days**. Data we must retain by law (section 6) is restricted, not deleted.

If you enter personal data in your own questions or brand/competitor details, it is sent to the AI services. Please avoid this unless necessary; if you do, we process it on your behalf under our [Data Processing Agreement](/avv).

## 6. Purchase, payment and invoice

Payment is handled via **Stripe Checkout** (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, Dublin, Ireland), which processes payment details, billing address, company name, VAT ID and email and issues the invoice. We do not receive full card or account details. We record when you confirmed buying as a business and which version of the Terms you accepted. Stripe is an independent controller for payment processing and fraud prevention ([Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy)); transfers to Stripe, Inc. (USA) rely on the Data Privacy Framework and SCCs. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR. Retention: invoices and accounting records **8 years**, books **10 years** (Section 147 AO, Section 257 HGB).

## 7. Emails

Only emails related to your check or purchase (confirmation, results ready, account verification, password reset, report PDF). About 8 weeks after a finished report we send **one** reminder that a follow-up measurement is available (existing-customer advertising, Section 7(3) UWG; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can **object at any time** via the unsubscribe link or by email; we keep unsubscribed addresses on a suppression list. Emails are sent via **Brevo** (Sendinblue SAS, Paris, France) as our processor; we do not use open or click tracking.

## 8. AI services

We send questions, brand/competitor names, domains and content of the checked public website – **not** your name, email or payment data – to: OpenAI Ireland Ltd (ChatGPT API with web search), Anthropic Ireland, Limited (Claude API with web search; also used for evaluation), Google (Gemini API with Google Search) and DataForSEO OÜ, Estonia (Google results pages incl. AI Overviews and AI Mode). They act as our processors and, under their terms, do not use the data to train models; they may retain requests for a limited time for abuse monitoring. Processing also takes place in the USA (Data Privacy Framework where certified, otherwise SCCs, Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, for third-party data on public pages Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Full list: [Sub-processors](/unterauftragnehmer).

## 9. Hosting

Hetzner Online GmbH, Gunzenhausen, data centres in Germany (processor). DNS via Cloudflare.

## 10. Usage statistics without tracking

We count steps such as "check started" or "report purchased" in our own database, linked internally to the check or account but without email addresses, and not shared. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Deleted or de-identified after **24 months**.

## 11. Cookies

Only strictly necessary cookies (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG): the session cookie (better-auth, up to 30 days or until sign-out) and `askmetry_locale` (language choice, 1 year). Plus Cloudflare Turnstile during the quick check.

## 12. No automated decision-making

We make no decisions based solely on automated processing within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Rate limits on the free check only prevent abuse.

## 13. Your rights

Access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and **objection** (Art. 21 GDPR) – including objection to direct marketing at any time without giving reasons. Contact: [hallo@askmetry.ai](mailto:hallo@askmetry.ai).

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Ours is: Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Ludwig-Erhard-Str. 22, 20459 Hamburg, Germany, [datenschutz-hamburg.de](https://datenschutz-hamburg.de).

## 14. Security and changes

TLS encryption in transit, password hashing, separation of customer data, least-privilege access. We update this policy when our service or the law changes; the version published here applies.
