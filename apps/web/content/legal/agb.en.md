## 2. What Askmetry measures

We read public pages of the given website, use AI to derive the brand profile, competitors and typical customer questions, put these questions to AI services (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode) and evaluate whether and how the brand is mentioned, compared with competitors, and which sources are cited. From this we derive recommended actions.
